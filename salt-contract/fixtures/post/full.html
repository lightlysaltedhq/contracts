Feeding your soil, not your plants

By Priya Shah 12 March 2026 4 min read

Most gardeners reach for a bottle of feed the moment leaves turn pale. The soil is usually the better place to start.

Why compost does the heavy lifting

A well-rotted mulch feeds the organisms that feed your plants, and it holds water through a dry spell.

Spread it two inches deep each autumn and leave the worms to do the digging. See our guide to hedge laying for the other end of the garden.
