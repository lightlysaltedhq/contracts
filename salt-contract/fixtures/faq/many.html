Questions

Before you book

The things clients ask us most often. If yours is not here, just ask.

How much does a first visit cost?

The first visit is free. We walk the garden with you, take notes and send a written quote within three working days.

Which areas do you cover?

We work across:

  • Bristol and South Gloucestershire
  • Bath and North East Somerset
  • North Somerset, as far as Clevedon
Do you use peat or chemical weedkillers?

No. We use peat-free compost and hand-weed or mulch instead.
