Restoring the walled garden

Hollin Farm

A derelict Victorian kitchen garden brought back into production for the farm shop and café.

Location
North Yorkshire
Duration

Year

The challenge

The walls were sound, but forty years of bramble and ash saplings had taken the beds, and the glasshouse had lost most of its glass.

What we did

We cleared the garden by hand to keep the old box edging, then laid out the beds again on the original plan.

Planting for the café

The planting follows the café's menu through the year, with salad and herbs nearest the kitchen door.
